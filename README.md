## 1. Introduction

Welcome to StudyAI ("we," "our," or "us"). StudyAI is an educational application designed to help students learn, practice, and improve their knowledge using digital learning tools and AI-powered features.

This Privacy Policy explains how StudyAI collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use our application.

By using StudyAI, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

## 2. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, StudyAI may collect the following information:

- **Account Information:** Name, email address, and authentication details when you create an account or sign in.
- **Learning Information:** Questions, prompts, quiz answers, study preferences, and learning progress.
- **Uploaded Content:** Text, images, documents, or photographs you choose to upload for educational assistance.
- **Device Information:** Device model, operating system, app version, diagnostic information, and crash reports.
- **Usage Information:** App interactions and feature usage needed to improve performance and user experience.
- **Purchase Information:** Subscription status and transaction-related information when you purchase premium features. Payment details are generally processed by the relevant payment provider.

We collect only the information necessary for the features we provide and the purposes described in this policy.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We may use collected information to:

- Provide AI-powered educational assistance.
- Answer study questions and explain academic concepts.
- Generate practice questions, summaries, and learning materials.
- Save learning progress and synchronize account information.
- Manage accounts and subscriptions.
- Improve app performance, reliability, and security.
- Detect technical problems and prevent abuse.
- Respond to user questions and support requests.

## 4. AI Features and Uploaded Content

When you submit questions, text, images, or documents to an AI-powered feature, that content may be transmitted to the AI service provider required to process your request.

The content is used to generate educational responses and provide the requested functionality. AI providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and contractual terms.

Please do not upload passwords, payment credentials, confidential documents, or other sensitive information that is unnecessary for your learning task.

AI-generated answers may occasionally be inaccurate and should be checked against reliable educational resources.

## 5. Camera and Photo Access

If StudyAI provides camera scanning or image-upload features, camera or photo-library access may be requested when needed.

Images are used for the feature you select, such as scanning a question or obtaining an explanation. We do not intend to access your camera or photos without the required permission.

You can manage camera and photo permissions through your device settings.

## 6. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share relevant information with service providers when necessary to operate StudyAI, including:

- **Google Firebase:** For authentication, database storage, or application diagnostics, if integrated.
- **AI Service Providers:** To process questions and generate educational responses.
- **Google AdMob:** To deliver and measure advertisements, if advertising is enabled.
- **Google Play:** To process and manage subscriptions or in-app purchases.
- **Technical Service Providers:** To maintain security, reliability, and app functionality.

These services may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable agreements. The services listed above apply only if they are actually integrated into StudyAI.

We may also disclose information when required by applicable law or when necessary to protect the security and legal rights of users or our service.

## 7. Data Storage and Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, loss, or disclosure.

Depending on the features used, information may be stored on your device or on secure servers operated by us or our service providers.

No method of electronic storage or internet transmission can guarantee absolute security.

## 8. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services, fulfill legitimate business purposes, and comply with legal obligations.

You may request deletion of your account and associated personal information by contacting us using the details provided below.

If account creation is available, StudyAI will provide an accessible account-deletion process in the app and, where required, through an external web resource.

Some information may be retained where legally required or necessary to resolve disputes, prevent fraud, or meet applicable obligations.
